# Retail Profitability Intelligence

**Power BI | DAX | Business Intelligence | Financial Performance Analytics**

## Project Overview

Retail Profitability Intelligence is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed with Microsoft Power BI to analyze commercial performance, profitability, sales trends, and business operations.

The project demonstrates the development of an end-to-end analytical reporting solution, from data preparation and relational data modeling to DAX calculations and interactive management dashboards.

Built using a synthetic retail business dataset, the solution enables users to explore revenue, gross profit, margins, product performance, and sales distribution across business channels and geographical regions.

## Key Features

- **Revenue Analysis:** Monitor net revenue, sales performance, and historical trends.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Evaluate gross profit, profit margins, and financial performance.
- **Year-over-Year Comparison:** Analyze revenue growth and changes between reporting periods.
- **Product Performance:** Compare sales and profitability across product categories.
- **Channel Analysis:** Evaluate revenue distribution across different sales channels.
- **Regional Performance:** Analyze geographical differences in commercial results.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Explore results using year and customer segment filters.
- **Executive Reporting:** Present financial and operational KPIs through interactive dashboards.

## Dashboard Structure

The Power BI report includes four analytical pages:

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a management-level summary of revenue, gross profit, margins, orders, sales trends, and business performance.

### 2. Profitability Drivers
Explores the components influencing profitability, including sales performance, costs, discounts, and returns.

### 3. Product Performance
Analyzes revenue and profitability across product categories to identify differences in commercial performance.

### 4. Channel & Region
Examines business performance by geographical region and sales channel, supporting comparative analysis.

## Data Model

The solution uses a relational analytical model designed to connect sales transactions with supporting business dimensions.

The model supports cross-filtering, financial KPI calculations, and comparative performance analysis.

DAX measures are used to calculate and display key business indicators dynamically.

## Getting Started

1. Download `Retail_Profitability_Intelligence.pbix`.
2. Open the file using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Refresh the data model if required.
4. Explore the four report pages.
5. Use the available filters to analyze performance by year and customer segment.
6. Review the KPI calculations and visualizations.
